INTERNAL CONTROL INSTITUTE GHANA (ICIG)
Building Stronger Organizations Through Effective Internal Controls
Internal Control Institute Ghana (ICIG) is the Ghana affiliate of the Internal Control Institute (ICI), USA, an international professional organisation dedicated to advancing knowledge, competence and professional practice in internal control.
ICIG promotes the understanding, development and effective application of internal controls across businesses, public institutions, not-for-profit organizations and professional practice in Ghana.
Our work is driven by a simple but powerful message:
INTERNAL CONTROL IS EVERYONE'S BUSINESS.
Internal control is not the responsibility of auditors alone. It is the responsibility of Boards, executives, managers, employees, entrepreneurs and every person entrusted with organisational resources, processes and responsibilities.

ABOUT ICIG
Who We Are
The Internal Control Institute Ghana (ICIG) serves as the Ghana affiliate of the Internal Control Institute (ICI), USA.
ICIG exists to promote the professional practice of internal control and to build the capacity of individuals and organizations to design, implement, assess, monitor and continuously improve their systems of internal control.
We provide a local platform through which professionals, organizations, students, business owners and other stakeholders can develop their understanding of internal control and connect with the broader professional standards, certification programmes and resources of the Internal Control Institute.
We believe that effective internal controls are fundamental to:
Good corporate governance
Effective risk management
Fraud prevention and detection
Regulatory compliance
Reliable financial and operational information
Protection of organisational assets
Accountability
Ethical behaviour
Operational efficiency
Sustainable organisational performance
ICIG therefore seeks to move internal control beyond the audit and finance functions and make it part of everyday organisational culture.

OUR VISION
To become Ghana's leading professional platform for advancing knowledge, competence and excellence in internal control.
OUR MISSION
To promote effective internal controls by developing professionals, supporting organisations, advancing professional certification and creating greater public awareness of the importance of strong controls in every sector of the economy.
OUR PURPOSE
We exist to help individuals and organizations understand one fundamental principle:
Good organizations do not leave control to chance.
Strong internal controls enable organizations to protect resources, manage risks, improve accountability and achieve their objectives.

OUR AFFILIATION WITH ICI USA
Connected to a Global Internal Control Community
The Internal Control Institute (ICI), USA is the parent professional body with which Internal Control Institute Ghana is affiliated.
ICI is dedicated specifically to the advancement of internal control knowledge and professional competence. Its activities include professional education, internal control resources, assessment tools and professional certification.
Through our affiliation, ICIG provides a Ghana-focused platform for promoting the principles, professional development opportunities and certification programmes associated with the Internal Control Institute.
ICIG supports Ghanaian professionals seeking to develop recognised competence in internal control while building a strong local community of practitioners committed to better governance, risk management and control.

PROFESSIONAL CERTIFICATIONS
Develop Recognised Expertise in Internal Control
Internal Control Institute professional certifications provide an opportunity for practitioners to demonstrate their knowledge, experience and competence in internal control.
The principal professional designations are:
Certified Internal Control Specialist — CICS
The Certified Internal Control Specialist (CICS) is a professional certification for individuals who wish to demonstrate competence in the principles and practice of internal control.
The certification addresses areas including internal control concepts, the control environment, risk management, control design, control assessment, governance and related professional competencies.

Certified Internal Control Professional — CICP
The Certified Internal Control Professional (CICP) is a senior-level professional designation for experienced practitioners who can demonstrate substantial knowledge and professional experience in internal control.
It recognises professionals who have developed significant practical competence in areas covered by the Internal Control Common Body of Knowledge.
The CICP is particularly relevant to experienced:
Internal Audit professionals
Risk leaders
Compliance professionals
Finance executives
Governance practitioners
Internal control specialists
Consultants
Senior operations professionals
Assurance professionals
Control and process improvement specialists

THE ICIG CERTIFICATION JOURNEY
Your Professional Internal Control Journey Starts Here
ICIG assists candidates in Ghana to understand and navigate the appropriate ICI certification pathway.
Step 1 — Submit Your CV
Send your current professional CV to ICIG for an initial assessment of your education, professional experience and internal control exposure.
Step 2 — Eligibility Review
Your background is reviewed to determine the professional certification pathway most appropriate for you.
Step 3 — Certification Application
Eligible candidates proceed with the relevant certification application and fulfil the requirements prescribed for the applicable designation.
Step 4 — Professional Development
Candidates participate in appropriate internal control learning and professional development activities to strengthen their practical understanding of internal control.
Step 5 — Code of Professional Conduct
Certified professionals are expected to uphold high standards of integrity, competence, responsibility and professional behaviour.
Step 6 — Continuing Professional Development
Professional competence does not end with certification. Certified professionals are expected to continuously develop their knowledge and remain current with developments in internal control, governance and risk management.

Internal control is often misunderstood as something belonging to auditors, accountants or compliance officers.
We want to change that.

Because controls happen everywhere.

A driver completes a vehicle inspection before beginning a journey.

A contractor follows established safety procedures.
A business owner separates personal money from business funds.
A manager reviews a transaction before approving it.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What is ICIG?
Internal Control Institute Ghana is the Ghana affiliate of the Internal Control Institute, USA. ICIG promotes internal control education, professional development, certification awareness and the advancement of good internal control practices in Ghana.
What is ICI?
The Internal Control Institute is the parent professional body dedicated to advancing professional knowledge and competence in internal control through education, resources and professional certification.
Does ICIG issue the CICS and CICP certifications?
The professional certification programmes are administered under the certification arrangements of the Internal Control Institute. ICIG supports candidates in Ghana through awareness, guidance, professional development and the appropriate application process.
What is CICS?
CICS means Certified Internal Control Specialist. It is a professional certification recognising knowledge and competence in internal control.
What is CICP?
CICP means Certified Internal Control Professional. It is a senior professional designation focused particularly on substantial professional experience and competence in internal control.
How do I know which certification I qualify for?
Submit your CV to ICIG for an initial review. Your education, professional experience and exposure to internal control activities will be considered in determining the appropriate pathway.
Is internal control certification only for auditors?
No.
Internal control is relevant to professionals working in finance, operations, risk management, compliance, IT, procurement, HR, governance, quality assurance, fraud risk management and many other functions.
Can organisations request customised training?
Yes. ICIG can provide customised internal control training and professional development programmes for Boards, management teams, employees and specialist functions.
